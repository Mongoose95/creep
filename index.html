Based on your folded-booklet samples, the calculator uses 2 × the measured sheet thickness for each nested-sheet step.

How to read the result

The center spread is the pair of pages shown when you open the booklet at its middle fold, where the staples go. For 36 pages, that is pages 18–19. The innermost sheet protrudes the most and gets the largest trim; each sheet after it gets 2 × one sheet-thickness less. The outermost sheet stays at 0 mm. Measure from the open edge opposite the fold/spine. Trim each folded sheet separately before assembling the booklet.

Geometric estimate for sheets of uniform thickness. Folding and compression may slightly change the result; test with a sample booklet before printing the full run.
